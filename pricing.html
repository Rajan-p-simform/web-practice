What Is Joint Genesis?

Joint Genesis is a daily joint-support capsule from BIODYNAMIX®, formulated by physician Dr. Mark Weis, M.D. It is built around hyaluronan, the molecule that gives your synovial fluid its thick, cushioning quality.

The idea behind it: in a remote Japanese farming village, seniors are said to stay remarkably flexible, and their diet is rich in hyaluronan-containing foods such as the purple sweet potato (satsumaimo). Because the body's hyaluronan levels fall as we age, Joint Genesis was created to support healthy synovial fluid, the "joint jelly" that lubricates and nourishes cartilage.

Supports joint lubrication

Mobilee® is a patented ingredient shown in studies to boost hyaluronan in the joints, helping rehydrate and thicken synovial fluid.
